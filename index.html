地图Maps

一级：Map of Principal Transportation Lines of the United States，Rand McNally 为基督教青年会学校印制，1921 年，美国国会图书馆藏。二级：The Rand-McNally New Commercial Atlas Map of West Virginia，1913 年。三级：美国地质调查局 1:62,500 地形图 15 幅，测印于 1909—1924 年（查尔斯顿、圣奥尔本斯、麦迪逊、佩托纳、蒙哥马利、诺格塔克、霍尔登、洛根、鲍尔德诺布、威廉森、马特万、吉尔伯特、派恩维尔、伊格、韦尔奇）。

地形图按其自带的投影参数换算后拼接，定位可靠。州图和全国图靠人工读取城镇位置配准：州图用薄板样条，核对过的城镇偏差在一两公里内；全国图误差约十公里。原图只做几何纠正与重采样，图面未作修饰；各图幅纸色不同是原貌。

Level I: Map of Principal Transportation Lines of the United States, Rand McNally for the United Y.M.C.A. Schools, 1921 (Library of Congress). Level II: The Rand-McNally New Commercial Atlas Map of West Virginia, 1913. Level III: fifteen USGS 1:62,500 quadrangles surveyed and printed 1909–24.

The topographic sheets are placed from their own projection parameters and are reliable. The state and national maps were registered by reading town positions by eye: checked towns on the state map fall within one or two kilometres; the national map is good to about ten. The maps are rectified and resampled only; nothing on their faces has been retouched, and the differing paper tones are original.

年表与史料匹配Chronicle and matching

事件日期主要依据 Laurie 1991 年论文、e-WV 百科词条和西弗吉尼亚州档案馆的概述，另有少数取自判词、国会记录和文献题名页。各家说法不一之处（人数、伤亡、战斗起止日）在条目里注明。地点坐标是城镇中心的近似值。

事件与史料页的对应是按关键词机械匹配的：同一页上出现了全部关键词，就截图并在词下画红线。这只说明该页提到了这些词，不表示内容已经核读，更不表示它支持某个论断。转录文本不是原件，引用须对照原刊。

Dates rest chiefly on Laurie (1991), the e-WV encyclopedia and the West Virginia State Archives' overview, with a few taken from court opinions, the Congressional Record and title pages. Where accounts disagree (numbers, casualties, the span of a battle) the entry says so. Coordinates are approximate town centres.

Events are tied to source pages by mechanical keyword matching: a page that contains every word of a keyword set is captured and the words underlined in red. That shows only that the page mentions them; the content has not been read for you, and no claim is implied. Transcriptions are not originals and must be checked against the printed source.
